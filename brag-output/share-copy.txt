Most RAG demos show you an answer. agentrag shows you where every quote came from — and
flags the ones it can't find. 152 public FDA and ClinicalTrials.gov documents, 13,423
passages, every citation located in the source before you see it. When the documents don't
cover something, it refuses instead of guessing.

The finding I didn't expect: on exact identifiers, dense embedding retrieval loses to plain
keyword search by 43–75×, depending on how the documents are split.
